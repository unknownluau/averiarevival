Creating an account on Averia requires verifying your Discord account.

To create an account, you must meet these requirements:
* You must be 13 years or older
* You must have a Discord account
* You cannot create more than **one account** on Averia without staff permission.
* You must have read and agreed to our Terms of Service and our Privacy Policy.

## Creating an account

1. Go to the [home page](/)
2. Click "Verify with Discord" to link your Discord account
3. Choose a username (3-20 characters, underscores allowed)
4. Choose a **secure, strong password** (minimum 8 characters)
5. Confirm your password
6. Complete the captcha and click "Sign Up"

Your account will be created immediately after signing up.
